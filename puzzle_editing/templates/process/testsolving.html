How to get your puzzle testsolved… (yay!!)

  1. Go to your puzzle’s page (find it under ✋ My Puzzles in the top menu). Next to the header Testsolve Sessions, click Start a New Testsolve Session.

  2. PuzzUp creates the session and a testsolve sheet in the Testsolving Drive folder. Put the date and time of your session in the Session Notes, or leave them blank if you don’t know yet.

  3. Copy the URL of the session page.

  4. Post the link with the date and time in our Discord (or poll for times). People will sign up. Aim for at least two solvers per session, and more if you can.

  5. If your puzzle can’t be tested in Google Drive (physical, audio, interactive…), ask c12 and they’ll help you work something out.

  6. Meet your solvers in the sheet at the agreed time and send them off to solve! Afterwards, read their feedback on the session page and revise.
