PuzzUp privacy policy

PuzzUp is a private tool used by a small puzzle-writing team to draft, edit and testsolve puzzles for a puzzle hunt. It is not offered to the public.

What PuzzUp accesses in Google

PuzzUp signs in to Google Drive and Google Sheets as one team-owned account. It uses that access only to:

  • create brainstorm, testsolving and factchecking spreadsheets in the team's PuzzUp folders;
  • read puzzle and solution Google Docs that team members choose to convert into hunt website pages.

It does not read, change or delete any other files, and Google data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

What PuzzUp stores

PuzzUp stores team members' account details (username, email and display name) and the puzzle drafts, comments and testsolve records they enter. This data is used only to run the tool.

Removing access or data

Google access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. Team members can ask the PuzzUp administrators to delete their account and data.
